Please order now — not at the deadline

The last couple of seasons, a lot of people waited until the last minute.

Late orders often cannot be placed at all

People who wait either run out of time to finish their order or try to place it after the club has already closed. Both cause a lot of confusion, and neither can be fixed after the fact.

Counting on buying extra caviar later is not reliable. Any surplus depends on the space left in our shipment boxes — there may be none, and at best it is around 10 boxes, which is nowhere near enough to go around.

About the club

This is a small site for the local caviar buying club in Mountain View. The idea is very simple: we order caviar, fish and seafood directly from the fishermen at Lummi Island Wild — more info here. Club news and other information are posted on this site.

Orders are usually placed up to a month in advance. After the club closes, delivery takes up to two weeks.

Joining the club

Two things to do: join the mailing group, and register on the buying-club site.

To join the club, sign up for our mailing group so you are notified about new orders. The group is also where members arrange splits, since some lots are too big for one household.

Do not order through www.lummiislandwild.com

www.lummiislandwild.com is now the retail website only. You cannot register for the buying club or place a club order there.

Make sure you are registered for the correct club

We have had mix-ups in the past with members ordering through another club by mistake. Use the Mountain View registration link above, and check the welcome message after you log in.

This season’s pricing

Two things changed in your favor, and one did not.

Shipping is now free

All club orders qualify for free shipping. There is no longer an additional shipping fee at checkout.

10% group discount

Our club gets a 10% group discount, so register and log in first to see discounted prices. What you see under our club is already 10% off the regular price, shown clearly in the cart during checkout.

Prices are up on many products this year

The grounds price of fresh fish more than doubled across all of Lummi Island Wild’s fisheries this year. Combined with higher fuel costs and increases in every part of processing, prices had to go up on many products. They have been kept as low as possible where possible.

Ikura processing costs in particular have risen substantially — consider this a heads-up before you open the order page.

Pickup day

Everything arrives deep frozen, and there is no freezer at the drop location.

Pickup address

821 E Dana St, Mountain View, CA 94041

Pick your order up as soon as possible after delivery

No frozen storage is available at the drop location, so you are responsible for collecting your order promptly. Check your email often on the delivery day.

The fishermen recommend bringing a cooler with ice so your caviar does not thaw on the way home.

If something looks wrong

I don’t see any products.
Products are only visible once you are logged in as a club member. Log in at liwbuyersclub.com and look for the “Welcome to Mountain View CA Caviar Buying Club” message at the top of the page.
It says my club is inactive.
Email sierra@lummiislandwild.com and ask them to fix it.
My old login doesn’t work at the new address.
It should — www.liwbuyersclub.com is the same website at a new web address, with the same registration details. Double-check you are not on www.lummiislandwild.com, which is the retail site.
